<h1>DashBoard Overview</h1>
<p>I created an interactive dashboard to translate the cleaned data into actionable business insights. Below is a breakdown of the key visualizations:

1. Revenue by Item (Bar Chart)
Purpose: Identifies the top-earning products for the cafe.

Data Source: Uses the recalculated Total Spent column to ensure 100% accuracy, excluding the original "ERROR" values found in the raw data.

2. Monthly Revenue Trend (Line Chart)
Purpose: Tracks financial performance throughout the year to identify seasonal peaks and valleys.

Technical Detail: This chart relies on the Month column I engineered during the data cleaning phase to filter specifically for 2023 data.

3. Average Price per Item (Bar Chart)
Purpose: Compares the price points across the menu to help with pricing strategy and menu optimization.

Data Source: Uses the cleaned Unit Price column, filtered to remove nulls and blanks.

4. Payment Method Distribution (Pie Chart)
Purpose: Analyzes customer behavior by showing the split between Credit Card, Digital Wallet, and Cash.

Business Value: Helps management understand which payment systems are most critical for the business.

5. Order Type Volume (Donut Chart)
Purpose: Compares the volume of In-store vs. Takeaway orders.

Business Value: Useful for staffing decisions and identifying if the cafe needs more seating or better packaging for takeout.</p>
